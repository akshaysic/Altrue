# Foundations · "Ledger"

**Status: proposed, not locked.** These come from the Claude Design canvas "Altrue core flow" (design direction v0). The design system will replace them once it's settled. Tokens live as CSS variables in `app/globals.css` and as `ds-*` classes in `tailwind.config.ts`.

The idea is Wise's approach, never its brand: the amount is the largest thing on every money screen, plain labelled rows sit between thin rules, there is one strong accent, and every surface is flat.

- **Compact amount:** 56 px, bold · `text-ds-amount-md`
- **Heading:** 32 / 36, bold · `text-ds-h1`
- **Row label and value:** 17 / 22, semibold · `text-ds-row`
- **Body:** 16 / 24 · `text-ds-body`
- **Secondary:** 14 / 20 · `text-ds-small`
- **Mono label:** 12 / 16 · `font-ds-mono text-ds-mono`

## Shape

- Pill buttons, 56 px high, full width at the bottom on mobile.
- Chips 48 px high. Every control is at least 44 px.
- Notices are flat tinted blocks with rounded corners.

## Never

Shadows, glows, gradients, floating cards, left-border accent cards, stock photos, emoji, and the legacy purple and magenta gradient.

## Copy

Calm and plain, with no guilt or urgency. Say "estimated" and "helped support", never "caused" or "guaranteed". Small amounts are valid. Copy in these stories is placeholder until it passes the two-vendor copy check.
