## Detail

### GiveDirectly
- Type: charity | Area: Poverty relief | Region: Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Malawi, DRC, Mozambique, US
- Sends cash straight to people living in extreme poverty by mobile money, no strings attached. Recipients choose how to spend it.
- **85%** of funds directly delivered to people in need (GiveDirectly homepage headline; the site also says more than 87% for its flagship program)
- **48%** lower infant mortality in a Kenya randomised study of $1,000 transfers (As reported by GiveDirectly on its own site)
- **2,248,119** people reached worldwide (GiveDirectly homepage counter)
- Evidence: Cash transfers are heavily studied (GiveDirectly cites 1,000+ studies). Long-term effects and spillovers are still being researched.
- Evaluator: GiveWell (top charity 2012 to 2022, not on the current list); Charity Navigator 99% four-star; CharityWatch Top Rated (as shown by GiveDirectly)
- Donate: https://donate.givedirectly.org
- Source: https://www.givedirectly.org/ (as of retrieved 2026-11-07)
- Note: Honesty note: GiveWell removed GiveDirectly from its top charities list in Aug 2022 after changing its criteria, and says this did not reflect a change in its view of the program. GiveWell says it still has a very high view of GiveDirectly. Monthly giving is offered. Strong candidate for the featured anchor.

### Malaria Consortium: seasonal malaria chemoprevention
- Type: charity | Area: Global health | Region: Sub-Saharan Africa
- Gives children preventive malaria medicine during the four months of the year when infections peak.
- **$7** to protect a child from malaria (GiveWell)
- **~$4,000** per life saved (estimated average for GiveWell funding in 2022 to 2024) (GiveWell estimate)
- Evidence: Exceptionally strong (GiveWell rating).
- Evaluator: GiveWell top charity; Giving What We Can recommended
- Donate: https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/charities/malaria-consortium
- Source: https://www.givewell.org/charities/top-charities (as of October 2026)
- Note: Malaria kills around 600,000 people a year, mostly children under 5 in sub-Saharan Africa (GiveWell).

### Against Malaria Foundation
- Type: charity | Area: Global health | Region: Sub-Saharan Africa
- Funds insecticide-treated nets that people sleep under, so they are not bitten by malaria-carrying mosquitoes.
- **$6** to provide one net (GiveWell)
- **~$5,500** per life saved (estimated average for GiveWell funding in 2022 to 2024) (GiveWell estimate)
- Evidence: Exceptionally strong (GiveWell rating).
- Evaluator: GiveWell top charity; Giving What We Can recommended
- Donate: https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/charities/against-malaria-foundation
- Source: https://www.givewell.org/charities/top-charities (as of October 2026)
- Note: Giving What We Can states $6 buys a net for 2 people for 2 years.

### Helen Keller Intl: vitamin A supplementation
- Type: charity | Area: Global health | Region: Areas modelled by GiveWell (country list not captured)
- Provides vitamin A supplements to children under 5. Deficiency leaves children open to infections that can be fatal.
- **$2** to give a child vitamin A for one year (GiveWell)
- **~$3,500** per life saved (estimated average for GiveWell funding in 2022 to 2024) (GiveWell estimate)
- **28%** of children in modelled areas have vitamin A deficiency (GiveWell estimate)
- Evidence: Very strong (GiveWell rating); results vary by study.
- Evaluator: GiveWell top charity; Giving What We Can recommended
- Donate: https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/charities/helen-keller-international
- Source: https://www.givewell.org/charities/top-charities (as of October 2026)

### New Incentives
- Type: charity | Area: Global health | Region: Northern Nigeria
- Pays cash to caregivers who bring babies to clinics for routine childhood vaccines.
- **$146** to vaccinate an infant (GiveWell)
- **~$4,500** per life saved (estimated average for GiveWell funding in 2022 to 2024) (GiveWell estimate)
- **38%** of infants in Nigeria did not get all recommended vaccines in 2025 (GiveWell)
- Evidence: Exceptionally strong (GiveWell rating), based on one high-quality study of the program.
- Evaluator: GiveWell top charity; Giving What We Can recommended
- Donate: https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/charities/new-incentives
- Source: https://www.givewell.org/charities/top-charities (as of October 2026)
